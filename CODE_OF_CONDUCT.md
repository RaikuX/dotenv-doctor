# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in the dotenv-doctor project — in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other channels — is expected to be respectful, constructive, and good-faith. We welcome contributors of every background and skill level.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient; assume competence and good intent
- Critique code, not people
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it charitably
- Keep discussions on-topic; take heated threads to private channels

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind
- Trolling, insulting, or demeaning comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sharing live secrets, credentials, or real `.env` values anywhere in project spaces
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for serious or repeated violations. Decisions can be appealed by contacting the maintainers directly.
